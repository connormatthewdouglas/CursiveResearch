## What Not To Overclaim

- No implemented system and no benchmark — treating the thesis as a demonstrated CursiveOS capability is unsupported.
- "Essential for ASI" is a philosophical position, not a proven theorem; the learnability criterion is hard to operationalize for hardware-scoped organisms.
- The corpus reading is **bounded** open-endedness only: novelty in preset space with frozen sensors and confirmation gates (Ch06/Ch08). Cite this paper when arguing *against* single-benchmark hill-climbing, not as evidence that open-ended self-modification is safe.
